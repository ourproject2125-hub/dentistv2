Today’s dental tip

Every six months for most people. If you have gum problems, diabetes, braces or a history of frequent cavities, every three to four months is better. Regular visits catch problems while they are small, quick and inexpensive to fix.

Not with modern technique. The pain people remember comes from the infection, not the treatment. With effective anaesthesia and rotary instruments, most patients say it feels like a long filling. Mild soreness for a day or two afterwards is normal and settles with regular painkillers.

  • Brush twice a day for two minutes with fluoride toothpaste.
  • Clean between teeth daily with floss or interdental brushes.
  • Limit sugary snacks and sweet drinks between meals.
  • Rinse with water after coffee, tea or betel.
  • Get a professional cleaning every six months.

Bring your child in by their first birthday or when the first tooth appears. Use a rice-grain smear of fluoride toothpaste until age three, then a pea-sized amount. Sealants on back teeth around age six greatly reduce cavities. Avoid putting children to bed with milk bottles.

Call us immediately for a knocked-out tooth, facial swelling, uncontrolled bleeding or severe pain that stops you sleeping.

If a permanent tooth is knocked out, hold it by the crown, rinse gently, and keep it in milk. Reaching us within an hour gives the best chance of saving it.
